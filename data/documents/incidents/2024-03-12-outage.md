# Incident: Retrieval Service Outage (2024-03-12)

## Summary

The retrieval service returned errors for approximately 40 minutes,
resulting in the answer service falling back to unanswered queries.

## Timeline

- 14:02 UTC: Alert fired for elevated 5xx rate on the retrieval service.
- 14:05 UTC: On-call engineer acknowledged and began investigation.
- 14:18 UTC: Root cause identified as a malformed document during ingestion
  causing the index writer to crash.
- 14:32 UTC: Bad document removed and ingestion re-run.
- 14:41 UTC: Error rate returned to baseline; incident closed.

## Root Cause

A source document contained invalid UTF-8 byte sequences that were not
rejected by the loader, causing a downstream encoding error during index
writes.

## Follow-Ups

- Add stricter validation to the document loader to reject files that
  cannot be decoded cleanly.
- Add an alert on ingestion failures, not just retrieval failures.
